Homotopy types of schemes notes

Notes from my mini-course on homotopy types of schemes at the August 2026 Melbourne/MATRIX conference on Future Directions in Higher Structures.

I'm an Assistant Professor at the University of Southern California.

My mathematical interests center around homotopy theory, algebraic geometry, and related topics.

In 2021, I finished my PhD in mathematics at MIT working with Clark Barwick. During the 2021–2022 academic year, I was an NSF Postdoc and UC President's Postdoc at UC Berkeley under David Nadler. During the 2022–2023 academic year, I was a postdoc at the Institute for Advanced Study under Jacob Lurie. From 2023 to 2025, I was back in Berkeley finishing my NSF postdoc.
